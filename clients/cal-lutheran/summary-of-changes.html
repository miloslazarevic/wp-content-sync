9. Link handling (corrected)

Applied This site runs behind a reverse proxy — WordPress lives at https://www.callutheran.edu/programs/, which is why the [home_url] shortcode exists. An earlier pass of this refresh preserved legacy root-relative /programs/… links instead of converting them. That was wrong, and has been fixed across every edited file:

  • 32 root-relative /programs/… links converted to [home_url]/… — including links in sections this refresh did not otherwise touch, such as the tuition page’s School of Management and Graduate School of Education tables and the untouched FAQ on the undergraduate listing.
  • Every outbound link opens in a new tab (target="_blank" rel="noopener") — including callutheran.edu URLs outside /programs/, which count as outbound because they leave this WordPress install. Two legacy bls.gov links were missing rel="noopener" and now have it.
  • [home_url] links, on-page anchors and mailto: links correctly do not open in a new tab.

Both rules are now written into clients/cal-lutheran/CLAUDE.md, so future rounds apply them automatically.
