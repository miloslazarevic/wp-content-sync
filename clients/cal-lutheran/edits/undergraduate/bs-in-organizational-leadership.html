Plenty of people start leading before anyone teaches them how. You do good work, people begin looking to you for answers and eventually you are responsible not only for your own performance, but for helping your organization do its best work too.

The Bachelor of Science in Organizational Leadership focuses on that human side of leadership and organizational improvement: coaching and motivating people, building workplace capital, communicating across differences, making values-based, ethical decisions, developing strong culture and helping organizations move through change.

Part of California Lutheran University’s Bachelor’s Degree for Professionals, this degree completion program is built for learners who have some previous college credit. You’ll learn in small classes with faculty who get to know you, value the experience you bring and support you as you earn your bachelor’s.

What You’ll Learn in the Organizational Leadership Degree

The program is grounded in the idea that leadership is a set of skills you can learn, with a foundation based on The Five Practices of Exemplary Leadership®. By graduation you’ll know how to:

  • Motivate teams through shared vision and leading by example
  • Recognize the strengths in the people you work with and empower them to act on those strengths
  • Engage and communicate effectively across different cultures and backgrounds
  • Use data and technology to improve the way an organization runs
  • Make values-based, ethical decisions that serve both the organization and the people in it

Who Is This Program For?

The BS in Organizational Leadership program is for people who want to finish their bachelor’s and turn their instinct into skill. They’re ready to lead others, or will become leaders in the future, and want to strengthen the human side of leadership.

Some of our students are team leads who were promoted because they were good at the work and now want to grow into the people side of the role. Others are preparing for their first leadership position and want to build skills in communication, motivation, conflict, coaching and change.

It’s also a strong fit for people in corporate roles centered on human behavior, like human resources, public relations and market research. These are fields you can enter and grow in without further licensing or an advanced degree, which makes a bachelor’s grounded in how people and organizations actually work a real advantage.

Students come from every kind of organization, including nonprofits, small businesses, large companies and fields with no obvious connection to business. The program gives them a place to study leadership directly: how people work together, how organizations change and how leaders earn trust.

What all applicants do need is a high school diploma or GED certificate and at least 24 credits of college credit from an accredited institution. Visit our admissions page for more on what’s required to apply.

Total Credits to Complete the Degree

To graduate, students must complete 124 credits minimum. This total includes:

  • Major courses: At least 48 credits are completed through organizational leadership major courses. Out of these, at least 39 credits must be upper-division courses (these courses are identified by course numbers in the 300 and 400 ranges).
  • General education and other courses: The remaining credits can consist of general education courses, additional major courses if desired and any transferred credits.

Frequently Asked Questions

A degree completion program is a bachelor’s degree built for people who already have some college credit. Instead of starting over, you transfer in the courses you’ve already earned and build your degree plan from there.

For the Bachelor of Science in Organizational Leadership, every incoming student brings at least 24 credit hours from a previous accredited associate or undergraduate program, and many bring more credits.

The difference from a traditional bachelor’s degree is mostly who it’s for and how it’s run — not the quality of the education. A general program is built around full-time students who are usually new to college. Cal Lutheran’s degree program is built around working adults who have some college behind them and a full life around them.

We know our students have to balance their degree around jobs, families and everything else, so the program runs fully online with the option to join a weekly live session in the evening.

Student support services are built for adult learners coming back to school too: academic advisors know your situation and personalized career counseling when you need it.

For someone who started a degree years ago and always meant to finish, a completion program is the most direct way back.

Business management focuses on how work gets done, including operations, project coordination, performance management, budgeting and decision-making. It is a good fit if you want a wide foundation in business or are preparing for supervisory roles that require you to understand how to juggle competing priorities across an organization.

Organizational leadership focuses more deeply on people. You’ll study how to motivate and coach teams, build trust, navigate conflict, set strategic goals, lead change management initiatives and make ethical decisions. It is a good fit if you are already leading others, preparing to step into a leadership role or want to better understand the human side of how organizations work.

If you want a broader business foundation, explore our BS in Business Management. If you want to focus on leading people and strengthening culture from the inside, organizational leadership may be the better fit.
