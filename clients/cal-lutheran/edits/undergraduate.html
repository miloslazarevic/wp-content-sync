Cal Lutheran’s Bachelor’s Degree for Professionals program includes six undergraduate degrees designed for adults returning to college with previous credits.

Each degree gives you a focused path toward completion, with courses that connect directly to the work people do in organizations, communities, schools, businesses and service fields.

Quick Facts

Classes meet once per week (for most courses)

Evening classes on Mondays – Thursdays

Small classes with ten students on average

Eight-week terms

Competitive tuition with multiple funding options

Explore Our Bachelor’s Degree Completion Programs

  • BS in Accounting and Financial Analysis | 100% Online
    Corporate accounting, financial information systems and the ethics of the profession, for people who want a career built on getting the numbers right.
  • BS in Business Management | Online or In-Person (Thousand Oaks)
    A broad foundation in operations, decision-making and process improvement for managers, owners and anyone whose job keeps adding responsibilities.
  • BS in Criminology and Justice Studies | 100% Online
    The justice system and the people who move through it, with as much attention to advocacy and re-entry as to law enforcement and the court system.
  • BS in Organizational Leadership | 100% Online
    How to motivate teams, improve culture and make ethical decisions an organization can stand behind, grounded in the human side of leadership.
  • BA in Psychological and Behavioral Science | Online or In-Person (Thousand Oaks)
    The major theories and research methods of psychology and how to apply them, in graduate school or a career that runs on understanding people.
  • BA in Strategic Communication | 100% Online
    Writing, speaking and critical thinking for the widening set of careers that depend on communicating clearly.

Who Should Consider Cal Lutheran’s Bachelor’s Degree Completion Programs?

Our programs are designed for:
  • Adult learners who are balancing education with work and family and have previously completed at least 24 credits from an accredited institution.
  • Working professionals seeking career growth and new opportunities.
  • Military veterans who can transfer up to 40 military credits.
With flexible evening and online classes, Cal Lutheran makes it possible to complete your degree on your terms. Visit our admissions page for more details on eligibility, credit transfers and how to get started.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • A degree completion program lets you build on your existing college credits to earn your bachelor's degree, instead of starting over.
  • To enter a degree completion program such as Cal Lutheran’s Bachelor’s Degree for Professionals, you need some existing college credits. We require a minimum of 24 credits to enroll.
  • Our program works well for working adults returning to college. We include support services specifically designed for these learners, including personalized academic advising, flexible coursework options, and career counseling.

  • On average, students can complete their bachelor’s degree in two years. The total time for each student depends on how many credits you are able to transfer into the program, the applicability of the credits to your degree of choice, and whether you take courses full-time or part-time.

All degrees in the Bachelor’s Degree for Professionals program are offered online. Some programs also offer an on-campus option at our Thousand Oaks location. You can find details for each program below.

  • BS in Criminology and Justice Studies: Online
  • BS in Organizational Leadership: Online
  • BA in Strategic Communication: Online
  • BS in Accounting and Financial Analysis: Online or on campus in Thousand Oaks
  • BS in Business Management: Online or on campus in Thousand Oaks
  • BA in Psychological and Behavioral Science: Online or on campus in Thousand Oaks

Students in the Bachelor’s Degree for Professionals must choose to either be an online student or an on-campus student. However, students who take the majority of their courses on campus can take up to 9 credit hours of online coursework.
