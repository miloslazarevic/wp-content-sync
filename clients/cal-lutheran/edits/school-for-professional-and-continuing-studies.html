In the School for Professional and Continuing Studies, you’ll find a distinctively different learning community that supports lifelong learners who want to return to their studies.

Our mission centers on investing in the academic, leadership and personal growth of our students. We encourage you to explore our adult degree completion programs and take the next steps to finish your degree.
