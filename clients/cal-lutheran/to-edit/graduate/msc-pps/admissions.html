MS in Counseling (PPS) Requirements

The MS in Counseling (PPS) is open to students from all undergraduate majors who have graduated from a regionally accredited college or university.

If you have any questions about applying to the master’s degree in school counseling, call 805-493-3325 or email clugrad@callutheran.edu to get in touch with a graduate enrollment specialist.

Application Checklist

  • Online application
  • Advising appointment with a graduate enrollment specialist (required)
  • Recommendation forms (2)
    • Cal Lutheran will send the required form to your two professional or academic recommenders. You can continue with your application without waiting for your recommenders to complete the form.
  • Official transcript(s) from a regionally accredited college or university
  • Personal statement
  • Verification of state-issued CTC Certificate of Clearance, Permit, or Credential
  • Application fee ($32)

Transcript Information

Official transcripts must be ordered and sent directly to Cal Lutheran via certified e-transcript or mail. E-transcripts are recommended where possible.

  • By email: If a selection list is available, please choose “California Lutheran University” or use clugrad@callutheran.edu for direct entry
  • By mail: California Lutheran University, 60 West Olsen Road, #2200, Thousand Oaks, CA 91360-2700
Domestic applicants who completed their bachelor's degree outside of the U.S. are required to submit an Official Transcript Evaluation from a CTC-approved foreign transcript evaluating agency.
